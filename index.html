About the modules

These modules accompany a 20-minute lecture for I301 students and connect directly to the AI Critique assignment. They open with one question: if an AI produces a clear, confident answer to your assignment question, how would you decide whether it is good?

The emphasis is on explaining judgments: what a response gets right, where it falls short, and how you would revise the prompt. Each module includes learning goals, core concepts, a worked example or short activity, a discussion question, and curated readings.
