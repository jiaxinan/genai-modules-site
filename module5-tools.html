📊Benchmarks and their limits

Stanford's 2026 AI Index technical-performance chapter shows how researchers compare AI capabilities. It reports both rapid performance gains and problems with evaluation reliability.

A benchmark measures performance on particular tasks under particular conditions. Its ranking cannot tell you everything about how a tool will perform on your assignment.

The response you receive depends on both the model and how you prompt it. Supplied information, enabled tools, and other settings also matter.

⚖️A simple comparison procedure

  1. Choose a task and define what a good response must accomplish.
  2. Try the same initial prompt and source material across available models.
  3. Evaluate the outputs with the same criteria (see Module 4).
  4. Revise the prompt and record whether performance changes.

Keeping initial conditions similar helps you compare models. Revising afterward shows what each can do with better instructions. Record the model name or version and the date, because these systems change.

For the AI Critique assignment, you may select any AI tool you prefer.

💬Think about it

For your critique, what matters more than an overall leaderboard position?

Possible answers

Accuracy on the assigned topic, appropriate evidence, and fit with the task.
